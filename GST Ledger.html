GST Accounting

GST in the Ledger

When you purchase, GST Paid sits as an asset — money owed back to you. When you sell, GST Collected is a liability — money owed to the ATO.

GST Collected = Liability (remit to ATO)
At BAS time, the business offsets GST Paid against GST Collected and remits only the net difference to the ATO. If GST Paid exceeds GST Collected, the ATO issues a refund.
