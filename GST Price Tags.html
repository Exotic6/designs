Extracting & Calculating GST

Two ways to work with GST: extract it from a price that already includes it, or calculate it on top of a base price. Same result — different starting points.

Both formulas confirm the same $20 GST. Use ÷ 11 when you have the GST-inclusive price; use × 0.1 when you know the base price before tax.
