Three Things to Remember About GST

GST is a pass-through tax. Understand these three rules and you'll never misrecord it again.

1
Rule 01
The Rate &
the Formula
GST is 10% of the base price. When a price already includes GST, divide by 11 to extract it.
÷ 11 to extract
2
Rule 02
Asset vs
Liability
GST Paid is an asset — claimable back. GST Collected is a liability — owed to the ATO. Neither one touches your profit.
Profit is unaffected
3
Rule 03
BAS &
Settlement
Each quarter, submit your BAS. Net GST (Collected − Paid) is either remitted to, or refunded by, the ATO.
Quarterly BAS
Summary
GST — Financial Accounting
in Action.
A 10% tax collected on behalf of the ATO, separated from revenue, offset at BAS time. Understand it and it takes care of itself.
Rate: 10%  ·  Extract: ÷ 11  ·  Add: × 1.1
GST Paid = Asset  ·  GST Collected = Liability
Net GST settled with ATO each quarter via BAS
GST applies to most goods and services in Australia at 10%. Businesses registered for GST must lodge a Business Activity Statement (BAS) quarterly or monthly to reconcile and settle their net GST position.
